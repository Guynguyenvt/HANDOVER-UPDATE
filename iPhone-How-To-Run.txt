HOW TO RUN HANDOVER APP ON IPHONE

1. On PC, open this folder:
   outputs\handover-app

2. Double-click:
   Start-Handover-App-iPhone.bat

3. Keep that black window open.

4. Make sure your iPhone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi.

5. On iPhone, open Safari.

6. Type the URL shown in the black window.
   Example:
   http://192.168.1.10:4175

7. To put it on your iPhone Home Screen:
   Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.

IMPORTANT:
- Keep the PC server window open while using the app.
- If Windows Firewall asks, allow Private networks.
- Your edits are saved in the browser on the device you use.
