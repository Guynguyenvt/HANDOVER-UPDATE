UPLOAD ONLINE FOR IPHONE USE ANYWHERE

Recommended simple way: Netlify Drop

1. On your PC, open:
   https://app.netlify.com/drop

2. Drag and drop this ZIP file:
   handover-app-iphone.zip

3. Netlify will give you a website link.

4. On iPhone, open that link in Safari.

5. Tap:
   Share > Add to Home Screen > Add

6. After that, open it from your iPhone Home Screen.

IMPORTANT:
- Your PC does not need to stay on.
- Your iPhone does not need to be on the same Wi-Fi as your PC.
- Your edits are saved in Safari/iPhone browser storage on that iPhone.
- If you clear Safari website data, saved edits can be removed.
